## What the brother-companies say

- Dangote Cement FY25: N4.31trn revenue, N1.01trn profit (23.6% net margin), ROE 42.3%, trades at 13.5x P/E, N45 dividend.
- Dangote Sugar FY25: N829bn revenue (+24.6%), N64.1bn LOSS (-7.7% net margin), N175bn finance costs - FX + debt can eat an entire Dangote business's margin.
- Refinery H1 2026: $14.4bn revenue, $1.8bn net income (12.5% net margin) after $1.51bn (2024) and $476m (2025) start-up losses; $3.65bn of debt.

The IPO price at N525 implies about 13.9x the refinery's annualised H1 2026
profit - almost exactly Dangote Cement's 13.5x. So the public-offer price
already assumes cement-like faith in a business with one half-year of
profits and $3.65bn of debt. The upside case earns DANGCEM-like returns;
the downside case is sugar-shaped.

Key risks not in the maths: the 30% greenshoe dilution, the private-placement
365-day lock-up expiry, FX liberalisation policy reversals, crude supply
scarcity from the NNPC's reduced stake, and cash conversion/Naira dollar-
remittance rules for the promised dollar dividends.

*This is an educational scenario model, not investment advice.*
