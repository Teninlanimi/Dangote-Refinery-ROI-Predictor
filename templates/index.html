What this model can & can't do

It projects per-share value from revenue × margin × P/E (benchmarks anchored on Dangote Cement FY2025 and Dangote Sugar FY2025, plus the refinery's own H1 2026 results), adds dividends and naira depreciation, and compares to the ₦525 offer. It does not model the 30% greenshoe dilution, the placement lock-up expiry, offer oversubscription dynamics, taxes, or refinery operating detail — and it is scenario analysis, not investment advice.
