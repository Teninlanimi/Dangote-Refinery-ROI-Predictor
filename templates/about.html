How this ROI prediction works

1. The pricing anchor

The Dangote Petroleum Refinery public offer is 4.1bn shares at ₦525 (SEC-approved, open 14 Sep – 27 Oct 2026, NGX listing expected Nov/Dec 2026), against 120.13bn total shares — an implied market cap of about ₦63.1tn (~$48bn at the ₦1,312.5/$ the prospectus itself implies).

The refinery only turned profitable in H1 2026 ($1.8bn net income on $14.4bn revenue ≈ 12.5% net margin) after $1.51bn (2024) and $476m (2025) of start-up losses. Annualised, H1 2026 gives roughly $3.6bn of profit a year — so the offer price implies about 13.4× annualised profit, almost exactly Dangote Cement's 13.5× P/E. That is the benchmark the offer is implicitly benchmarking itself against.

2. The three scenarios come from the sister companies

Since the refinery has one half-year of profit history, the scenarios are benchmarked off the two Dangote refineries that do have full listed track records:

Every Dangote business that IPO'd in Nigeria has ended up somewhere on that cement↔sugar spectrum; the point of the model is to price both ends.

3. The maths

  • Fair value = net income × P/E ÷ 120.13bn shares.
  • Growth: earnings compound at the scenario rate (capacity ramp toward the 1.4m bpd 2029 target, petchem).
  • Dividends: from year 2, payout % of profit ("dividends in dollars" has been promised, not yet signed off).
  • Naira: exit price converts at a depreciating rate (default −8%/yr). ROI is shown in naira and USD.
  • Expected ROI = probability-weighted across the three scenarios (25% / 50% / 25%).

4. What the numbers said at launch

On the 3-year horizon with defaults: bull ≈ +208% (₦) / +145% (USD), base ≈ +42% (₦) / +13% (USD), bear ≈ −70% (₦) / −76% (USD), probability-weighted ≈ +55% (₦) / +23% (USD). Your own levers may differ — that is the point of the sliders.

5. Honest limits

  • No greenshoe (up to 30% extra shares) or lock-up-expiry dilution is modelled.
  • Refinery taxes, crude-supply politics (NNPC's stake), and pet-chem splits are not separated.
  • The margin/P/E ladder is a deliberately simple linear model — real markets re-rate fast.
  • All inputs are public-reporting figures as of 13 Oct 2026; check ipo.dangote.com and the prospectus for authoritative numbers.
⚠️ This is an educational scenario model, not investment advice. Equities can and do lose money; an IPO price is not a fair value. Do your own research (and read the prospectus) before investing in anything.
