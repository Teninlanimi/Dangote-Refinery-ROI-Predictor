What each check does

  • Range: Brent must be between $10 and $250 a barrel, petrol and diesel between $0.50 and $8 a gallon, and the naira between ₦300 and ₦5,000 per dollar. Anything outside is rejected and never reaches the model.
  • Jump: a move of more than 15% (Brent), 20% (petrol, diesel) or 10% (naira) from the previous saved value is kept but flagged for review.
  • Freshness: flagged if the newest oil price is more than 7 days old, or the newest exchange rate more than 4 days old.
  • Fetch: the source couldn't be reached or sent nothing back. The other sources still run.
