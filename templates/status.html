Check rules

range — Brent must sit between $10 and $250, petrol and diesel between $0.50 and $8 a gallon, USD/NGN between ₦300 and ₦5,000; anything outside is rejected and never reaches the model. jump — a move over 15% (Brent), 20% (petrol, diesel) or 10% (naira) from the previous stored value is kept but flagged. freshness — flagged if the newest oil-market price is over 7 days old or the newest FX rate over 4 days old. fetch — the source could not be reached or returned nothing.

Sources: Brent, petrol and diesel spot prices from FRED (US EIA data); USD/NGN from Rates By Exchange Rate API.
